The Data Practice contract proposal for £156 million received approval following comprehensive evaluation. The contract will enable procurement of specialist data roles on a temporary basis, aligning with the data strategy and hub and spoke operating model. Olive Jar emerged as the preferred supplier after demonstrating satisfactory responses to commercial queries and legal review. The arrangement includes approximately 85% subcontracting to Scrum Connect. Directors requested enhancement of future contract presentations to Digital ET to ensure clearer articulation of commercial terms and value propositions.
The ADAMS contract valued at £37.1 million received approval for a four plus one-year term. This arrangement will provide essential support for residual systems following ADEP contract exit, delivering £7.5 million in savings over the five-year period compared to current costs. The contract secured a competitive application development rate reduction of 77% to just over £200 per day. Directors emphasized the importance of developing a more strategic approach to future supply chain management.
Discussion of SR Run assumptions addressed multiple factors affecting Digital's cost base. These included increased service digitisation demands, enhanced cybersecurity requirements, and the ongoing modernisation of legacy systems. Directors examined the challenges of achieving efficiency targets while managing rising costs associated with expanded digital service provision. The dialogue emphasized the importance of articulating Digital's value proposition through measurable service performance improvements and departmental efficiency gains. Consideration focused on workforce planning requirements, particularly regarding the transition from contingent to permanent labor and development of specialist capabilities aligned with the Department's 2030 strategy.
The Secretariat outlined arrangements for the upcoming Worsley Innovation Hub visit, including the morning tour and afternoon DET sessions on data strategy and legacy technology debt. Directors were asked to provide suggested topics for the operational frontline engagement session to ensure productive dialogue.
